Privacy policy: Godspire: Ascension of the Myths

Last updated: 21 October 2026
Who we are: Steven Foyston, stevefoyston@gmail.com

The short version

Godspire keeps your game on your phone. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number and we do not run our own analytics. The game offers optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob, which use your device's advertising id (see below).

What stays on your phone

Your progress, settings and purchase receipts (order numbers) are saved in a file on your phone. If you use Android's own backup, Google may back that file up to your Google account under Google's terms. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes the file.

Services the game uses

  • Google Play. Purchases are made and processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We receive an order number and a purchase token for each purchase. We also send Google Play a random number made when you first install the game, so that Google can tell which install a purchase belongs to. It is not your name, email or account.
  • Google AdMob (ads). The game offers optional videos that you can watch for a small bonus. Watching is always your choice, and holders of the Premium Pass do not see them. To show ads, Google AdMob uses your device's advertising id and other device information to choose and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under Google's own privacy policy. Where the law requires it (the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent first and does not request an ad until you agree. You can change this later in Settings (Ad privacy choices), and you can reset or delete your advertising id in your Android settings.
  • Google ML Kit. If you turn on chat translation, the translation runs on your phone. Language packs are downloaded from Google the first time you use a language. See Google's ML Kit privacy information for what Google may receive when packs are downloaded.

Children

Godspire is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for players aged 16 and over. It contains optional purchases, including random items, and publishes its odds in the game.

Your choices

You can delete all game data by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. You can turn notifications and translation on and off in Settings. Spending is only ever started by you, and Google Play lets you set purchase approvals and budgets on your account.

Changes

If the game starts collecting data (for example accounts or cloud saves), we will update this policy and the game before it does.
